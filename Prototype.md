Description:
    1st prototype of the weekly display of events beign schedule to the calender. 
    Shows the events that currently schedule to for this weeky by day and time slot and allows for the input of new events to th calender. The events will display red on calender when there is an overlap or time conflict with another event. There is also a message above the calender that tells what events are conflicting.
